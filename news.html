Recent work 21 September 2026

No two technology visits are ever quite the same.

Over the last few weeks, I’ve been helping people across the local area with all sorts of everyday technology. Some visits have involved fixing something that has stopped working, while others have focused on explaining technology and helping somebody feel more confident.

  • Sorting out unwanted apps, suspicious messages and mobile-phone problems
  • Replacing routers and reconnecting broadband, printers and other devices
  • Updating laptops, preparing them for Windows 11 and securely setting up a new business laptop
  • Configuring Microsoft 365, business email and protected cloud storage
  • Fixing television, remote-control and regional-channel problems
  • Reconnecting heating controls following a broadband change

One recent visit took me slightly further afield than usual, where I set up a new printer so it could be used from both a laptop and phone. We also spent time going through a Kindle and building confidence using it.

That patient, one-to-one side of the work is something I’m genuinely passionate about. Nobody should feel embarrassed about asking a question or needing something explained again.
