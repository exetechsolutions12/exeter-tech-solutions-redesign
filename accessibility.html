Accessibility

Support and information that are easier to use.

I want the website and service to work for as many people as possible.

My aim: to make the website and support service usable, understandable and welcoming for people with different needs, devices and levels of digital confidence.

Using this website

The website has been designed with clear headings, plain language, readable colours, visible keyboard focus, labelled form controls and layouts that adapt to phones, tablets and computers. Important information is not hidden behind animation, and motion is reduced when your device requests it.

You should be able to navigate with a keyboard, use browser zoom, increase text size and use common screen-reading or voice-control software. The site aims to follow the principles of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA, although this is an ongoing process rather than a claim that every possible combination of technology has been tested.

Built-in display options

An Accessibility button appears at the bottom-right of every page. It can increase the text size, switch to a higher-contrast display, add extra text spacing, underline links or reset your choices. Your selection is stored only in your browser and is not sent to Exeter Tech Solutions.

The main menu also includes a website search. Search results are generated from a local list of public pages, without a third-party search provider. The search has a visible label, works with a keyboard and announces its result count to assistive technology.

You can also use your browser’s own zoom controls. On most Windows computers, press Ctrl and + to zoom in and Ctrl and 0 to reset. On a Mac, use Command and + or Command and 0.

Adjustments to technology support

If you need information explained more slowly, repeated, written down, displayed at a larger size or provided in another practical format, mention this when making an enquiry. Appointments can include breaks, step-by-step notes and time to practice. You are also welcome to have a family member, friend, carer or support worker present.

Third-party services

The enquiry form uses Formspree and Cloudflare Turnstile for delivery and protection from automated submissions. If either service is difficult to use with your technology, you can make the same enquiry by email, phone or text instead.

Report an accessibility problem

If something on the website is difficult to access, please email info@exetertechsolutions.co.uk, call or text 07480 256100, and describe the page and problem. I will try to provide the information another way and improve the website where possible.
