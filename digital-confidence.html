Digital confidence

Patient Digital Support in Exeter & South Devon

Friendly one-to-one help for anyone who wants to feel more confident using a phone, tablet or computer. I support older people, people living with disabilities and anyone who feels uncertain or overwhelmed by technology, with patient guidance at your own pace.

At your pace

The session is built around you.

You choose what you would like to learn. We can focus on one immediate task or work through several everyday skills during the visit.

There is no judgement and no expectation that you should already know what to do. I can repeat steps, help you practise them yourself and provide simple written notes where helpful.

A trusted person is welcome

A relative, friend, carer or support worker can be present if that makes the person receiving support feel more comfortable.

What a visit feels like

  • Your questions guide the session
  • Explanations in plain English
  • Time to practise the steps yourself
  • Support adapted to your needs
  • No jargon, judgement or rushing

Visits from £45

Up to one hour at your home. The price is agreed before booking and may vary by location.
