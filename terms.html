Customer information

Terms and booking information

The key information to understand before authorising work.

About the service and contact details

Exeter Tech Solutions is the trading name of sole trader Cameron Cater. Support is available by prior arrangement for home users, community organisations, sole traders and small businesses.

You can contact us by phone on 07480 256100 or by email at info@exetertechsolutions.co.uk.

Quotes and authorisation

The expected work, price and support method will be agreed before work begins. If further work, parts, paid software or subscriptions are required, these will be discussed before any additional charge is incurred. A diagnosis cannot guarantee that a device or data can be repaired or recovered.

Appointments and access

Support is currently provided at your home; devices are not taken away or accepted for drop-off. Please provide safe and reasonable access to the relevant equipment. Appointment times may occasionally need to change because of travel disruption or an earlier job taking longer than expected; you will be contacted as soon as practical.

Cancellations

Consumers who book at a distance or away from business premises will normally have a 14-day cancellation period. If you ask for work to begin during that period, you may be asked to confirm that request and may need to pay a reasonable amount for work completed before cancellation. Once a service has been fully performed, the cancellation right may end where the required acknowledgement has been given. Specific cancellation information will be included with the booking authorisation.

Your files and accounts

Please back up important information before support where reasonably possible. Although reasonable care will be taken, technology work can involve risks such as hardware failure, corrupted data or unsuccessful recovery. You remain responsible for maintaining appropriate backups unless a separate backup service has been expressly agreed.

Passwords and payments

You should not disclose passwords, PINs or one-time security codes. If support involves banking or payment apps, you must remain in control of the device and authorise any action yourself. Exeter Tech Solutions provides technical guidance, not financial advice.

Payment

For home visits, payment is normally due at the end of the appointment and can be made by card, cash or secure payment link. Residential customers are not normally offered payment by invoice. Approved business customers may be offered 14-day invoice terms by prior agreement. Parts, paid software, subscriptions and other third-party costs may require payment in advance.

Website design and managed website services

Website projects are confirmed through a separate written proposal setting out the pages, features, content responsibilities, price and payment schedule. Website ownership and handover arrangements will also be confirmed before work begins. Unless otherwise agreed, the customer is responsible for ensuring that supplied text, photographs, logos and other materials may be lawfully used.

Customers may choose to manage the completed website themselves or subscribe to a managed website plan. The managed plan may include hosting, SSL, administration of a standard domain renewal, basic maintenance, backups and occasional small content updates. New pages, substantial redesigns, additional functionality, premium domains, business email and paid third-party services are not included unless expressly agreed.

The domain will be registered for the customer and remains the customer’s property. Exeter Tech Solutions may administer the domain while the managed plan remains active and payments are up to date. The project-specific website-service agreement will explain renewal, cancellation, transfer and what happens to the website and domain when managed support ends.

14-day follow-up

If the same issue returns within 14 days of the completed work, please make contact and it will be reviewed without an additional labour charge. This does not cover unrelated faults, hardware failure, third-party services, customer changes, new software or later updates. This follow-up policy does not affect your statutory consumer rights.

Problems and complaints

If something has not gone as expected, email info@exetertechsolutions.co.uk with the job details, or write to Cameron Cater trading as Exeter Tech Solutions at the postal correspondence address above. The issue will be reviewed and a practical response provided as soon as possible. Nothing in these terms affects your statutory consumer rights.
