My background

Technology should make life easier, not leave people behind.

Experience

I have more than seven years’ experience in IT and digital support and currently work in digital transformation within NHS primary care. My day to day work includes solving technical problems, helping people access digital services, training staff and turning complicated instructions into clear, manageable steps.

Why I started Exeter Tech Solutions

I started Exeter Tech Solutions because I've seen first-hand how frustrating and overwhelming technology can be, especially when people are left feeling confused or unsure who to turn to for help. I've always enjoyed helping people with technology, and I wanted to turn that into something that genuinely makes a difference in my local community. For me, it's not just about fixing problems. It's about taking the time to listen, explain things in plain English and help people feel more confident and independent. Nobody should ever feel embarrassed about asking for help, no matter how simple the question might seem.

Who I support

I provide patient, practical technology support to people who need a little extra help with technology, from individuals and families to elderly people, those living with disabilities, small businesses and community organisations. Whether you're looking for help yourself or arranging support for someone else, I'm here to make technology easier to understand and use.
