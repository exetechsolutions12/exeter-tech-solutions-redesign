Help at home

Home IT Support in Exeter & South Devon

I provide friendly, practical home computer support across Exeter and South Devon. From slow computers and Wi-Fi problems to printer setup, phones and new devices, I can help you get things working again in the place where you use them.

You do not need to know what is wrong or understand the technical details. Just explain what is happening, and we will work through it together.

You do not need to diagnose it

Just explain what is happening.

Whether something has stopped working, a new device needs setting up or you simply want somebody to show you what to do, you can describe the problem in ordinary language. If you would like more time to learn how to use your technology at your own pace, you can also explore my digital confidence support.

Most everyday support can be covered during a home visit. I’ll discuss the likely work, confirm the price and agree a suitable appointment before anything is booked.

Home visits from £45

Up to one hour of practical support. Additional time is £20 for each further 30 minutes and is discussed before continuing.
