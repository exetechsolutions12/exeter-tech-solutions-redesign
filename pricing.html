Clear, fair and agreed in advance

Simple pricing.

No confusing packages or surprise charges. I’ll confirm the most suitable option and price before any work begins.

Before work begins: fixed prices are used wherever practical. Parts, paid software, subscriptions and other third-party costs are not included unless clearly agreed. Exeter Tech Solutions is not currently VAT registered, so VAT is not added.

Home visit

From £45

Up to one hour at your home. This can include computer health checks, email, software, accounts, Microsoft 365, new-device setup, Wi-Fi, printers and general troubleshooting. Home visits can also include help with virus or malware removal and transferring files to a new device; I’ll discuss what’s needed and confirm any additional costs before work begins.

The price covers the booked visit of up to one hour and is not reduced if the work takes less than the full hour. It is confirmed before booking and may vary by location. Additional time is £20 for each further 30 minutes.

Digital confidence visit

From £45

Up to one hour of patient, one-to-one help with phones, tablets, computers, apps, email, video calls, accessibility settings, online forms, scam awareness and everyday digital tasks.

You choose what you want to learn. There is time to ask questions, repeat steps and practice, with simple written notes where helpful.

The price is confirmed before booking and may vary by location. Additional time is £20 for each further 30 minutes.
