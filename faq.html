IT Support FAQs for Exeter & South Devon

Find answers about home visits, digital-confidence support, small-business IT, pricing, payments and what to expect when you book.

What devices and systems can you help with?

I can help with Windows laptops and desktop computers, iPhones, Android phones, iPads and Android tablets, printers, home Wi-Fi, email, Microsoft 365, apps, online accounts, cloud storage and everyday digital tasks. If your issue needs a specialist hardware repair, I’ll explain that before any work is agreed.

Where are home visits available?

Home visits are available in Dawlish, Dawlish Warren, Teignmouth, Starcross, Exminster, Newton Abbot, Exeter and selected nearby areas. See the home IT support service for more details. Include your town or postcode area when enquiring so I can confirm whether the journey is practical and whether the standard £45 starting price applies.

Do you take devices away or accept drop-offs?

Support is normally provided during an agreed home visit. Where longer setup work is better completed off site, collection and return may be arranged under a written handover and authorisation agreement. Unarranged device drop offs are not accepted.

What does the £45 home visit include?

The standard home visit includes up to one hour of support. You can also see the full visit pricing. This can cover computer health checks, email, software, accounts, Microsoft 365, new-device setup, Wi-Fi, printers, general troubleshooting or patient digital-confidence guidance. The price covers the booked visit and is not reduced if the work takes less than the full hour. Additional time is £20 for each further 30 minutes. Some specialist work, such as malware removal or data transfer, may be priced separately and will always be agreed before work starts.

Will you need my password or PIN?

I’ll normally ask you to enter passwords and security codes yourself. If agreed off-site work requires temporary access, this will be documented, details will be supplied separately and any temporary record will be securely deleted after the device is returned.

How can I pay?

Home-visit customers can pay by card or cash at the end of the appointment, or by a secure payment link. Residential customers are not normally invoiced. Approved business customers may be offered payment by invoice with 14-day terms by prior agreement. Parts, paid software and other third-party costs may require payment in advance.

When will you respond?

I aim to reply to enquiries within one working day.

What if I need to cancel or rearrange an appointment?

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice where reasonably possible. If something unexpected happens at shorter notice, contact me as soon as you can and I’ll try to rearrange the appointment. This practical request is separate from, and does not affect, any statutory cancellation rights explained in the terms and your booking information.

What if the problem cannot be fixed?

I’ll explain what I found and recommend the most practical next step. Time spent diagnosing or attempting an agreed repair may still be chargeable, but I’ll explain this before you authorise the job.

What if the same problem comes back?

If the same issue returns within 14 days of the completed work, contact me and I’ll review it without an additional labour charge. This does not cover unrelated faults, hardware failure, third-party services, customer changes, new software or later updates. This policy does not affect your statutory consumer rights.

Are parts and paid software included?

No, unless they are specifically included in the agreed quote. I will discuss replacement parts, paid software, subscriptions or other third-party costs with you before purchasing or adding them.

Can you help a small business?

Yes. Pay-as-you-go support is available for sole traders and small organisations, including Microsoft 365, email, shared documents, websites, staff guidance and everyday digital systems. Explore the small-business IT support service. The scope and price will be agreed before work begins.

Do you work with community organisations and charities?

Yes. Digital-confidence sessions, small-group guidance and informal technology drop-ins can be arranged for community organisations, charities, social groups and supported-living settings. Find out more about community support. The format and price are agreed with the organisation in advance.
