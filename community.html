Local community support

Helping more people feel included and confident with technology.

The idea

Local support that meets people where they are.

Many people need more than a quick technical fix. They need time to ask questions, practice at their own pace and understand how technology can help them stay connected and access everyday services.

I want to work with community groups, charities, social groups, supported-living settings and other local organisations to make that support easier to access.

Sessions can be shaped around the people attending, the devices they use and the support an organisation already provides.

Possible session topics

  • Phones, tablets and everyday apps
  • Email, messages and video calls
  • Online forms and useful services
  • Accessibility settings and adjustments
  • Recognising scams and staying safer online
  • Building confidence through guided practice
Flexible formats

Support designed around the community.

The right format will depend on the venue, the people attending and what they would find most useful.

Small-group sessions

Friendly guidance built around a shared topic, with time for questions and practice.

Informal drop-ins

People can bring a device or question and receive straightforward, practical guidance.

Community partnerships

One-off sessions, regular support or a longer programme developed with a local organisation.
