Your information

Privacy notice

How personal information is collected, used, shared and protected.

In short: I use only the information reasonably needed to answer enquiries, arrange and provide IT support, take payment, keep business records and deal with problems. I do not sell personal information or use it for unrelated advertising.

Who is responsible for your information?

Cameron Cater, trading as Exeter Tech Solutions, is the data controller for the personal information described in this notice. The business is based in Dawlish, Devon and provides services across Dawlish, Dawlish Warren, Teignmouth, Starcross, Exminster, Newton Abbot, Exeter and selected nearby areas.

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, email info@exetertechsolutions.co.uk or call 07480 256100.

Information I may collect

  • Your name, email address, telephone number if you provide one, and town or postcode area.
  • Enquiry, booking and service details, including your preferred support method, appointment information and correspondence.
  • Information about the device, software, account or technical problem for which you request help.
  • Job notes, work authorised, work completed, quotes, invoices, receipts, payment status and service history.
  • Complaint, claim, security-incident or follow-up information where relevant.
  • Limited technical information produced by the website host, such as IP address, browser information and security or server logs.

Please do not send passwords, PINs, one-time security codes, full card details or unnecessary sensitive information. During support I may see information displayed on your device or account. I will only access what is reasonably necessary for the authorised work, will not record it unless needed, and will ask you to enter passwords and security codes yourself wherever possible.

How I obtain information

Most information comes directly from you through the website form, email, booking or support appointment. Some payment and transaction information may be supplied by SumUp or my bank. Technical information may be supplied by the website and email providers. If another person books support for you, I may receive your contact and appointment details from them and will confirm the arrangement with you where appropriate.

I do not currently send general marketing emails. If that changes, this notice and the way preferences are collected will be updated before marketing begins. Where consent is used, you may withdraw it at any time.

Do you have to provide information?

You do not have to make an enquiry or provide optional details. However, I need enough contact, booking and problem information to respond, agree the work and provide the service, and I must keep certain payment and accounting records by law. If the necessary information is not provided, I may be unable to arrange or complete the requested support.

Special-category information

I do not intentionally collect information about health, ethnicity, religion, political views, trade-union membership, genetics, biometrics, sex life or sexual orientation. It may occasionally be visible incidentally while working on a customer’s device. Please close unrelated files and applications before support. I will avoid accessing or recording such information unless it is genuinely necessary and a lawful condition applies.

Children’s information

Services are booked and authorised by adults. I do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through this website. If support concerns a device used by someone under 18, the booking and any necessary decisions should be handled by a parent, guardian or other responsible adult.

Who information may be shared with

I share only what is reasonably necessary. Recipients may include:

  • Cloudflare, which provides website hosting, content delivery and security services. Cloudflare Turnstile helps distinguish genuine visitors from automated submissions and may process limited technical and security information such as IP address, browser or device characteristics and interaction signals.
  • Formspree, which receives website-form submissions, makes them available through its dashboard and sends enquiry notifications to the business email account.
  • 123 Reg and its service providers, which provide the domain and business email services.
  • SumUp, which securely processes card and contactless payments. I do not receive or store your full card number.
  • Banking, accounting, insurance, legal or professional advisers where needed.
  • Government bodies, regulators, law-enforcement agencies or other organisations where disclosure is legally required.

I do not sell or rent personal information. Service providers may act as processors under their terms and data-processing agreements; some, such as payment providers, may also act as independent controllers for their own legal and regulatory purposes.

Small-business customer data

If a business customer asks me to access personal information that it controls, such as employee or customer information, I will act only on its instructions for the authorised support. Where required, the respective data-protection responsibilities and processor terms will be agreed separately. This notice still applies to the business contact and billing information I use for my own administration.

International transfers

Some service providers or their subprocessors may process information outside the UK, including in the United States. Where this involves a restricted transfer, the provider and/or Exeter Tech Solutions must use a lawful safeguard, such as UK adequacy regulations, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to approved standard contractual clauses. Further information about the relevant safeguard can be requested by email.

Security

I use reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to a small IT-support business, including secured devices and accounts, access controls, multi-factor authentication where available, software updates, secure payment processing and procedures for lost devices, misdirected emails and compromised accounts. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to be informed; access your information; correct inaccurate information; have information erased; restrict its use; object to processing; receive certain information in a portable format; and withdraw consent where consent is relied upon. These rights are not absolute and legal record-keeping requirements may apply.

To make a request, email info@exetertechsolutions.co.uk. I may need enough information to confirm your identity. There is normally no fee, and I will usually respond within one month.

I do not use personal information to make solely automated decisions or for profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

Complaints

Please contact me first so I can try to resolve your concern. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, telephone 0303 123 1113, or write to Information Commissioner’s Office, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire, SK9 5AF.

Cookies and website data

This website uses only essential functionality by default. Optional Google Analytics is available to help Exeter Tech Solutions understand how people use the website, such as which pages are visited and how visitors move around the site. Google Analytics is not loaded unless you choose Accept analytics in the cookie controls. If you reject analytics, the Google Analytics tag is not loaded. You can change your choice later using the Cookie settings link in the website footer. Your analytics choice is stored locally in your browser so the site can remember it.

Google Analytics is provided by Google and may process information about your device, browser and use of this website after you opt in. Exeter Tech Solutions uses this information to understand website performance and improve the service, not for behavioural advertising. If you use the display controls, choices such as larger text, higher contrast, extra spacing or underlined links are also stored locally in your browser and are not sent to Exeter Tech Solutions. The website search uses a list of public pages held within the site and does not use a third-party search provider. Search words can appear in the page address and browser history, so please do not enter personal or sensitive information. Cloudflare and Cloudflare Turnstile may process technical information and use strictly necessary security storage or cookies to identify automated or malicious activity. Formspree processes the information entered into the enquiry form and technical information needed to deliver and protect the submission.
